Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Felty is designed to let people express a feeling without creating a personal profile. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate, understand, secure, and improve the service, and we do not require an account, name, or email address to use the core features of Felty.

1. Who We Are

Felty is a mobile application operated by Felty. In this Privacy Policy, “Felty,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the Felty service and its operator.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at: privacy@felty-app.com

2. Information We Collect

Information you do not need to provide

Felty does not require you to create an account and does not require your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location to use the core app experience.

Device Identifiers

Felty and/or integrated third-party services may process device-related identifiers, such as an advertising or app-related device identifier, where this is enabled by the app configuration and permitted by your device settings.

Usage and Product Interaction Data

We may collect information about how you interact with Felty, such as which features or feeling buttons are used, basic interaction events, and information needed to measure and improve the app.

Feeling selections and aggregate counts

When you select a feeling, such as Ghosted, Valued, Unvalued, Cheated, Got Cheated, Love Bombed, or Succeeded, Felty may process that interaction so that the corresponding aggregate count can be displayed.

Felty is designed around aggregate statistics rather than public user profiles. We do not ask you to attach your name or identity to a feeling selection.

3. Sharing a Felty Card

Felty allows you to create a visual card representing a selected feeling and share it using the iOS share sheet or other apps such as WhatsApp, X, or other supported services.

When you choose to share a card, the sharing action is controlled by you and the destination service you select. Felty does not receive the private messages, recipients, or conversations that you create in those third-party services.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed through Felty may be used to:

  • provide and operate the Felty service;
  • maintain aggregate feeling counts;
  • understand feature usage and improve the app;
  • monitor performance, reliability, and security;
  • detect, prevent, and investigate abuse or technical problems;
  • comply with legal obligations; and
  • where applicable, support advertising or measurement activities in accordance with your permissions and applicable law.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Felty may use analytics or measurement technologies and may process device identifiers for purposes disclosed in the App Store privacy information.

Where Apple requires permission for tracking through App Tracking Transparency, Felty will request the appropriate permission before engaging in tracking covered by that framework. You can manage tracking permissions in your Apple device settings.

We do not use tracking to identify you personally from your feeling selection, and we do not sell your personal information.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

Felty may use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate parts of the app, including backend/database infrastructure, analytics, security, hosting, and measurement services.

Backend Infrastructure

Felty may use Supabase to provide backend and database infrastructure. Information sent to the backend is processed only as necessary to provide the relevant Felty functionality and according to the applicable service terms and privacy practices.

Third-party providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to use it only for the services they provide to Felty, subject to their applicable agreements and legal obligations.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain aggregate statistics, improve and secure Felty, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Because Felty is designed to minimize directly identifying information, some aggregate information may not be reasonably capable of being associated with a particular user.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

You can also manage Apple permissions, including tracking permissions, through your device settings.

To make a privacy request, contact: privacy@felty-app.com

We may need enough information to verify and process a request. If information has been irreversibly aggregated or cannot reasonably be linked to you, we may not be able to identify or delete that aggregate information.

9. Children's Privacy

Felty is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request names, contact information, or other directly identifying information from children as a condition of using the core app.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Felty in a way that should not have occurred, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

10. International Data Processing

Depending on the services used to operate Felty, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live. Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through Felty against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Felty's features, data practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

13. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy can be sent to:

privacy@felty-app.com
