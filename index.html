Privacy Policy · Last updated: April 2026

1. No Data Collected

Felty does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, or any identifying information.

2. No Account Required

Felty requires no registration or login. You can use the app completely anonymously.

3. No Tracking

Felty does not use analytics tools, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs that monitor user behavior.

4. No Cookies

Felty does not use cookies or any similar tracking technologies.

5. Local Storage Only

All data generated within the app is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any server.

6. Children's Privacy

Felty does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes

If we update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

8. Country Detection

Felty detects your country to show regional statistics. This is performed via ipapi.co using your IP address. Felty does not store your IP address at any point. Only your country code is used and immediately discarded after detection.
